# Current Fallout campaign rules

Current through Session 1, release 2.1.0. This document and SPECIAL clarifications supersede conflicting wording in the bundled base conversion PDF. The economy PDF and 668-item source roster remain the economic authority. No Star Wars currency/prices or Force rules are imported.

## Latest accepted rules

- P061: Awareness is the renamed Perception skill for noticing, searching and detecting threats. It remains governed by SPECIAL Perception (underlying Cunning). Existing ranks, career status, XP costs, talent references and training credit transfer unchanged. Vigilance remains a separate skill. Historical source references to the Perception skill mean Awareness.

- All otherwise unspent Advantage outside combat recovers 1 strain each unless the player specifies otherwise. Includes the end-of-encounter recovery check; replaces, does not stack with, its older two-Advantage rate. Minimum strain 0. In combat, the player chooses spends.
- After an encounter, make one Simple Cool or Discipline check: recover 1 strain per Success, plus the default above. Adequate full sleep clears strain; a session boundary or drink does not.
- Session XP: 5 for management/downtime; 10–15 for significant combat/objectives; 20–25 for exceptional sessions; 25 gameplay maximum. No XP per die roll, kill or message. Training awards stay in separate ledgers. Session 1 awards 10 unrestricted XP once; no purchases made.
- SPECIAL is doubled display only. Blade Technique uses Agility for checks AND Melee damage contribution. Both Piloting skills use Perception. See [SPECIAL clarifications](special.md).
- Personal Destiny refreshes at gameplay-session start only. No shared pool, flip, reload refresh or session-end refill.
- Distinct Personal/Vehicle range bands; weapon range scale and damage scale are independent. Power armor silhouette 2, personal movement, vehicle Armour/hull. Each 10 Pierce ignores 1 Armour; lesser Pierce does not. After Armour blocks 10 damage per point, positive remainder rounds UP to whole hull trauma (Armour 1: 11–19 damage causes 1 hull trauma).
- Track loaded ammunition separately from shared reserves. Ordinary attacks spend their ammo cost even on a miss; no ammo was spent from Ronin’s recovered guns during Session 1.

## Adopted training, injury and recovery framework

The authored adoption text below remains operative except where the explicit current amendments above supersede older wording. It is a conversion summary, not the complete SGR or EotE sourcebook.

Fallout EotE Conversion - v2.0

Campaign edition | 22 November 2026

This edition adopts the agreed training, injury, recovery and equipment-condition framework adapted from Standard Galactica Ruleset (SGR). These are Fallout campaign rules, not official EotE rules. EotE remains the baseline where this document makes no replacement. The separately published Fallout Campaign Economy remains the pricing authority.

Rules priority: this edition's campaign amendments supersede conflicting earlier conversion text. SGR itself is a design source, not an automatic import of every rule, price, setting assumption or GM instruction.

Character creation retains normal EotE costs and the six existing characteristics, adding Luck as a seventh characteristic starting at 1. Raising Luck uses the existing starting XP budget at new rank x 10; no extra starting XP is awarded. Maximum Luck is 6. Native synth construction does not impose a purchased-implant Luck penalty.

Ronin: Brawn 2, Agility 3, Intellect 2, Cunning 2, Willpower 3, Presence 2, Luck 1. His original 110 XP remains 60 in characteristics and 50 in talents. Blade Technique uses Agility for Melee checks and the characteristic contribution to Melee damage. Melee 2 gives two Proficiency and one Ability; his katana has base damage 5.

Talent grids retain vertical progression: any top-row talent may be bought; deeper talents require the one directly above. No horizontal or diagonal unlocks. Ranked instances are separate purchases. Dedication grants its stated increase directly, without an additional training gate.

Retained foundations: silhouette-2 power armor, whole penetrating hull trauma rounded upward, suit physical characteristics, tracked ammunition, daily stimpak diminishing returns, existing radiation and chem dependence. The bundled base conversion preserves those foundations; the sections below supply the adopted systems.

Not adopted: Force powers, SGR adjacency, armor-bypassing component shots, Multi-Scale damage, monthly stimpak resets, automatic conversion of Star Wars prices to caps, or SGR's narrative launcher ammunition.

Luck and personal Destiny

Each individually statted character starts with Luck 1. Destiny maximum equals current Luck; remaining points refresh to that maximum at session start, not at midnight or each training month. Spent points disappear. They never transfer or flip to an opponent. Rivals and Nemeses each have their own pool; minions and minion groups have none. There is no shared player pool or general GM pool.

Spend one point to upgrade your check, upgrade an incoming attack's difficulty, introduce a plausible narrative detail, or activate a talent requiring Destiny. For this campaign, one point can instead reduce your check's difficulty by removing one purple Difficulty die. This is a reduction, not a red-to-purple downgrade; it cannot remove a Challenge die. Retain the normal once-per-side-per-check limit. State pre-roll uses before rolling.

Training exception: after seeing a training roll, spend one point to add one Success to the raw result and recalculate cancellation. This does not guarantee success or cancel Despair. It replaces your pre-roll Destiny use on that check; it is not an extra spend after already using a point.

No skills derive from Luck. Luck training XP can only improve Luck. Raising it follows characteristic training below. A newly raised maximum does not refill spent points mid-session; the extra capacity fills at the next session refresh. A reduced maximum caps remaining points immediately.

Purchased cybernetic augmentation costs 1 effective Luck per implant, minimum 0; base Luck before implant penalties limits implant count. Track base Luck, implant penalties and effective Luck separately. Further characteristic advancement raises base Luck at its normal new-base-rank cost. It can offset penalties; it does not create a separate cheap effective-rank advancement track. Ronin's native synth body carries no implant penalty.

Obligation retains the existing campaign's EotE procedure. The SGR growing solo-Obligation system is not adopted by these amendments. No injury, training failure or dependence automatically adds Obligation.

Record Luck and Destiny remaining/maximum on the companion. NPC pools remain GM records. Session refreshes and spends are recorded by the GM; the player companion remains read-only.

Downtime training

Declare one skill or one owned specialization before a training block. Each month requires living costs and access to appropriate practice, materials and instruction where necessary. Time passes for NPCs, pursuits, businesses and events. Interruptions can end or postpone a block; do not award a full month's training for a brief interrupted attempt.

Holistic: 3 training XP per successful month, compatible with employment and recovery. Focused: 5 XP per successful month, no active income. Dangerous: 10 XP per successful month, genuine hazards and no active income. Managed assets may operate at every tier. At block end award an additional 1 unrestricted session XP per completed month, even on failed training months.

Roll the trained skill, or an associated specialization skill agreed before training. For tree training use the higher of the target talent's row and the highest row already acquired. For skill training use the target rank. The following pools are complete base opposition (P = Difficulty, R = Challenge):

Target 1: Holistic automatic; Focused 1P; Dangerous 2P.
Target 2: Holistic 1P; Focused 2P; Dangerous 3P.
Target 3: Holistic 1R 1P; Focused 1R 2P; Dangerous 1R 3P.
Target 4: Holistic 2R 1P; Focused 2R 2P; Dangerous 2R 3P.
Target 5: Holistic 3R 1P; Focused 3R 2P; Dangerous 3R 3P.

Keep three ledgers: unrestricted session XP; training XP locked to a named skill/tree; and characteristic training XP locked to a named characteristic and its normally associated skills. Never transfer training XP between these ledgers. Skill and talent purchases retain EotE costs and campaign prerequisites. No monthly point awards bypass prerequisites.

Normal gameplay awards remain 5 XP for management/downtime sessions, 10-15 for substantial objectives/combat, and 20-25 for exceptional sessions; 25 is the gameplay award ceiling. Training awards are recorded separately. Do not manufacture new sessions just to refill Destiny during a continuous training block.

Training outcomes and characteristics

A passed monthly check earns the tier's training XP; a failed or tied check earns none. If the player chooses to spend them this way, two net Advantage bank one Boost for the next training roll in that block, expiring if unused. Otherwise unspent Advantage follows the outside-combat strain-recovery default. Triumph doubles earned training XP once; it does not award XP on a failed check. Multiple Triumphs do not multiply it again.

Despair applies even on success: Holistic earns 0 XP; Focused suffers 1 wound per Despair; Dangerous suffers 2 wounds and one critical injury per Despair. On a failed Dangerous check also suffer 2 wounds per net Failure and 1 wound per net Threat. Threat on a successful check causes no extra training wounds. Training wounds bypass soak. Resolve each critical in sequence, including existing-injury modifiers. Treat actual injuries before continuing hazardous practice.

Characteristic training is a separate block with no concurrent skill/tree training or active income. Roll the current unmodified personal characteristic as Ability dice, without a skill. Target ranks 2-6 require that many Difficulty dice for Focused training, or that many Challenge dice for Dangerous training. Holistic characteristic training is unavailable. Earn 5 or 10 characteristic XP per successful month, respectively, with the same outcome rules above.

Purchase costs new rank x 10. At least one successful training check for this characteristic and target rank is required before purchase; combine its banked XP with session XP to pay. The new rank then improves future training pools. Dedication is a separate route and needs no training gate. Temporary chems and suit ratings cannot qualify as permanent characteristic development.

Characteristic XP may buy that characteristic or skills normally governed by it. Blade Technique does not reclassify Melee for XP spending: Melee remains in the Brawn training ledger. Luck has no derived skills. Maximum natural characteristic rank remains 6.

Applicable facilities, an active mentor, materials or a useful training partner can add Boost. Exceptional instruction may upgrade instead of granting a Boost for the same benefit. Poor supplies or disruption add Setback. Do not count one circumstance twice. Two months devoted to a single attempt may remove one purple Difficulty die; it cannot remove a red die and does not double that attempt's training XP. Both months still incur expenses and grant their 1 session XP each.

Injuries and training restrictions

Track wounds, critical injuries and temporary effects separately. Critical injuries persist until treated even when their immediate combat effect expires. Each unresolved critical adds +10 to later critical rolls. Healing wounds does not remove these records, and stabilization is not a cure.

Use the EotE critical table and normal activation requirements. Each hit can cause one purchased critical; additional activations increase its roll by +10. Vicious, Lethal Blows and existing-injury modifiers apply normally. Crossing from at/below the wound threshold to above it causes the normal automatic critical. Add the uncapped accumulated wounds above threshold as an excess-damage modifier to that automatic roll only. Do not add it to every unrelated later critical.

Example: threshold 12, already 8 wounds, then 9 wounds after soak produces 17 total. The threshold-triggered critical adds +5 excess damage, plus other applicable modifiers. Against a suit, vehicle damage remains hull trauma; it does not become occupant wounds merely to trigger this rule.

Treatment uses Medicine against the critical's listed severity. Each critical permits one treatment attempt per week; an earlier retry needs a specifically established advanced treatment facility/procedure, not merely another doctor. Success clears the injury's unresolved-critical modifier. Permanent losses still require replacement, rehabilitation or an explicit restorative procedure; ordinary healing does not regrow limbs.

Record: injury name, date, severity, present effect, effect expiry, untreated/stabilized/healed status, treatment attempts and next eligible attempt. An expired combat effect does not spontaneously recur. If a lingering symptom is appropriate, establish its trigger and mechanical consequence when recording the injury, not retroactively during a later encounter.

Training restrictions apply only when the injury interferes with the activity. For affected Focused or rolled Holistic training: add 1 Setback while wounded below half threshold, 2 at/above half; add 1 per relevant critical. Holistic target-rank-1 training remains automatic if the activity is feasible. An impossible activity must be replaced or postponed.

For affected Dangerous training: each wound adds 1 Setback; every complete three wounds also upgrades opposition once; each relevant critical adds one more upgrade, without also applying its Focused-training Setback. These are severe risks, declared before commitment. Apply the injury's normal effects where relevant without counting an identical penalty twice.

Recovery and medical care

Strain: after an encounter, make a Simple Cool or Discipline check. Recover 1 strain per net Success and 1 per otherwise unspent net Advantage under the player’s outside-combat default; a Triumph's included Success counts once, with a separate appropriate benefit. During ordinary checks, one Advantage may recover one strain where spending is applicable. Adequate full sleep restores all strain. Stimpaks do not heal strain.

Wounds: adequate nightly rest restores 1 wound. Holistic training permits natural healing. Focused training prevents it only when the activity aggravates the injury or prevents adequate rest; establish this before the block. Dangerous training prevents natural healing during active training. Shelter, food, water and uninterrupted sleep matter; months of untreated dangerous practice are not free recovery time.

Stimpaks: retain 5/4/3/2/1/0 wound recovery for successive doses, with diminishing returns resetting after 24 hours under the existing EotE rule. No calendar-month reset. A ready dose takes one maneuver to administer. Track consumed doses and supplies. Stimpaks do not cure critical injuries, radiation or dependence.

First aid: retain EotE Medicine difficulty and limits rather than SGR's blanket Average difficulty. A patient at or below half wound threshold is Easy, above half is Average, and above threshold is Hard. Each Success heals one wound; each Advantage heals one strain. One wound-healing attempt per patient per encounter. Appropriate medical equipment is required to avoid the usual equipment penalties; no automatic generic medkit bonus is added by this amendment.

Revival: wound healing that brings a wound-incapacitated character to or below threshold restores consciousness unless another condition prevents it. On their next turn they have no action and may take only one maneuver, costing 2 strain; incidentals remain available. If unable to voluntarily suffer that strain, they cannot take that maneuver. Normal turns resume afterward. Wound healing does not reverse strain incapacitation.

Bleeding Out: retain its immediate wound/strain and further-critical consequences until stabilized. A successful first-aid Medicine action, using the greater of ordinary wound-treatment difficulty and the injury's severity, can stabilize bleeding even if wounds remain high. Healing to or below wound threshold also stabilizes it. Stabilization stops the immediate bleed but does not heal the critical or restore consciousness by itself. Do not restart bleeding without an established trigger or new injury.

Radiation and dependence retain the original conversion rules. Ordinary food, sleep and medical wound treatment do not remove Rads. Clinical services replace bacta narratively; no automatic bacta-equivalent healing rate or Star Wars price is imported. Infiltrator synths receive ordinary biological treatment.

Equipment, assets and implementation

Adopt a separate equipment-condition record: undamaged, minor, moderate, major, destroyed. Good used equipment can remain undamaged. Cosmetic age alone does not force degradation. Sunder, explicit damage and established breakdowns change condition; do not add random wear every attack.

Personal weapons: minor adds 1 Setback; moderate upgrades difficulty once; major does both; destroyed is unusable. Replace rather than cumulatively stack these check penalties. Personal armor loses 1/2/3 points of its armor-provided soak at minor/moderate/major, minimum zero contribution; moderate also disables one stated armor quality, major all such qualities. Never subtract biological Brawn or talent soak. Destroyed armor gives no protection.

Repair one step at a time: minor to undamaged Average Mechanics; moderate to minor Hard; major to moderate Daunting; destroyed to major Daunting upgraded once, only if recoverable. Missing tools/facilities add appropriate Setback. Success restores that step. Materials use 5%, 15%, 30% or 60% of the intact item's established campaign value respectively; specialist labor uses the existing Fallout economy service basis. These percentages are campaign estimates, not game-confirmed prices. Quote time and total cost before work; do not import SGR's flat credit labor fees.

Vehicles and power armor keep their existing hull, system strain and critical tracks. Track structural condition separately when an established incident damages the structure. Personal-armor soak-loss rules do not reduce vehicle Armour. Do not import SGR's blanket condition drops at both thresholds or its ship repair tariffs. The current vehicle repair rules remain authoritative until a specific condition effect is assigned to a vehicle profile.

Assets can include property, vehicles, power armor and managed enterprises. Training expenses include agreed food, shelter, instructors, supplies and maintenance. Managers may run assets during training; no free profits are invented for an asset without established income, operating costs and a named manager. SGR's business tiers and territorial resource economy are optional future modules, not silently active rules.

Record personal NPC disposition separately from faction reputation. Establish factions, affiliations and relationships through play; unrelated independent groups share no automatic reputation. Numerical SGR reputation cascades are deferred, rather than imported without a Fallout faction model.

Cloning and synth production remain specialist setting infrastructure. Price and establish body production, templates, facilities, staff and training separately when a project enters play. Imprinted information does not automatically grant trained skill ranks, judgment or a restored player identity. This is not a resurrection entitlement.
