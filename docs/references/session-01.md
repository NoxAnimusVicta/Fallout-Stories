# Session 1 — The road to Hamilton

Ronin encountered trader Luis Ortega and his daughter Elena fifteen miles east of Hamilton. Two armed escorts were threatening them over a letter from Elena’s aunt in Ruby Valley Refuge. Ronin fought and disarmed both raiders. After the fight, Luis and Elena decided the men could not be allowed to return to their gang. Luis killed the surrendered rifleman; Ronin killed the unconscious second guard. The bodies were moved into the roadside drainage ditch.

Ronin returned Elena’s letter and recovered 25 caps, a hunting rifle and a .38 pipe handgun with ammunition. Luis said that Pike and his men take what they want in Hamilton: travellers can pay and leave, but residents cannot. Luis offered a meal and somewhere to sleep. His intended explanation is that the escorts died trying to rob a traveller.

Ronin cleaned his blade, covered the firearms’ exterior markings with dirt, drank some offered water and set off with Luis, Elena and their loaded brahmin. The session ends at departure, with about fifteen miles and six hours still ahead—not at Hamilton. No training or XP purchases took place.
