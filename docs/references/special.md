# SPECIAL presentation — release 2.0.5

SPECIAL scores are twice the established EotE characteristic ratings. This is the requested Fallout presentation conversion, not a rule found in the supplied Standard Galactica Ruleset. Dice pools, damage, soak, thresholds, XP costs and training mechanics remain unchanged.

| SPECIAL | Existing rating | Ronin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | Brawn | 4 |
| Perception | Cunning | 4 |
| Endurance | Willpower | 6 |
| Charisma | Presence | 4 |
| Intelligence | Intellect | 4 |
| Agility | Agility | 6 |
| Luck | Luck | 2 |

Use half the displayed score for existing characteristic-based calculations. An increase of one existing rating displays as +2 SPECIAL; the existing ordinary maximum of 6 displays as 12. No odd-score advancement or new point-buy system is introduced. Skill ranks are unchanged. Resilience retains its existing Strength association; the Endurance rename does not move skills or recalculate thresholds. Blade Technique still changes accuracy only.

Destiny capacity is half displayed Luck: Ronin has 1 point. The supplied SGR, Luck & Destiny Points section (lines 3054–3083), explicitly refreshes personal pools at session start, without transfer or flipping. A reload, new chat, day of rest or training month does not create a new gameplay session. The player accepted retaining this rule after checking the source. No refresh was awarded during this update.

Ronin starts with 20 caps by player instruction; this is starting equipment allocation, not earnings from an invented encounter.

The vertical list is inspired by the New Vegas Vigor Tester. Brief current-rank labels are verified against [New Vegas SPECIAL](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Fallout%3A_New_Vegas_SPECIAL); other ratings use descriptive fallback labels. The explanatory sentences describe this campaign's mechanics, not New Vegas's derived-stat formulas. Labels are flavor and confer no modifiers.

Phone icons are opaque RGB PNGs with blue #164B7A and yellow #F6CF45, served under new filenames. iOS may retain artwork for an existing Home Screen installation; replacing that shortcut may be necessary to receive the new icon.
