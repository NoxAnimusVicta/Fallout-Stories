# SPECIAL and talent clarifications — current through Session 1

SPECIAL scores display twice the retained EotE ratings. This is the approved Fallout presentation, not an SGR doubled-stat rule. Use half displayed scores for dice, damage contributions, prerequisites and XP calculations. Skill ranks remain unchanged; maximum ordinary rating 6 displays 12.

| SPECIAL | Underlying characteristic | Ronin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | Brawn | 4 |
| Perception | Cunning | 4 |
| Endurance | Willpower | 6 |
| Charisma | Presence | 4 |
| Intelligence | Intellect | 4 |
| Agility | Agility | 6 |
| Luck | Luck | 2 |

Both Piloting Air and Piloting Surface use Perception (underlying Cunning); Ronin rolls 2 Ability untrained. Resilience still uses Strength. Renaming Willpower to Endurance does not reassign skills or recalculate thresholds.

Blade Technique may use Agility for Melee checks AND the characteristic contribution to Melee damage. Ronin’s katana is 3 Agility+2 weapon bonus=5 base damage, plus uncancelled successes. Melee2 gives 2 Proficiency+1 Ability. Brawl, soak, carrying capacity and thresholds are unchanged. This damage extension is approved homebrew beyond Ataru Technique, which substitutes Agility for Lightsaber checks. [Ataru reference](https://imadeyoursite.com/Edge/trees/Seeker%20Full%20Career.pdf).

Deadly Accuracy: Melee is passive on every successful Melee attack. Add trained Melee ranks to one hit’s damage, not to every hit of a multi-hit attack. It has no daily/encounter/round limit or activation cost. Ronin has NOT acquired it or Dodge.

Talent grid prerequisites run vertically: buy the talent directly above before the next row. Ronin owns B1/B2/C1/C2/C3/D1 internally: Quick Draw, Grit, Jump Up, Blade Technique and two Parry ranks, costing 50 starting XP. No new talent purchases occurred in Session 1.

Destiny capacity is half displayed Luck: 1. Pools refresh at gameplay-session start, not at session end, app reload, calendar day or training month; spent points do not flip or transfer. The SGR Luck & Destiny section is the source. Session 2 has not started.

Personal and Vehicle ranges are distinct. Vehicle Close contains personal ranges; broader vehicle bands suit aircraft and heavy weapons. Speed governs vehicle movement. Mounted personal weapons retain personal range; damage scale does not determine range. Power armor retains personal movement and silhouette 2.

Rank descriptors are flavour, inspired by the [New Vegas SPECIAL tester](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Fallout%3A_New_Vegas_SPECIAL); they confer no mechanical benefits.
